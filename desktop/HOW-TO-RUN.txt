EJC Slope Stability: desktop app for Windows
============================================

1. Unzip the download and put EJC-Slope-Stability.exe anywhere you like (Desktop, Documents, a USB stick).

2. Double-click EJC-Slope-Stability.exe.
   The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because the program is not signed with a
   certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". An antivirus may also ask: the program only serves the
   app's files to your own browser, on this computer.

3. A small window opens, and the app opens in your web browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox...).
   Keep the small window open while you use the app. Close it to quit.

Good to know
- Everything runs on your computer and nothing is uploaded. The app works without internet (only the fonts look
  slightly different offline).
- "Save case" and "Report" save files where your browser saves downloads. "Open case file" opens a saved case, also
  one saved from the web page or the Streamlit app.
- Your browser remembers your inputs for the next time.
- The shared case list exists only on the claude.ai page. Here, send a case file or a case code instead.
- Starting the program again while it runs just opens the app again.
- SHA256.txt holds the program's checksum, if you want to check the download.

Research tool for the prescribed-surface comparison in Mahat (2026) thesis; not for design.
