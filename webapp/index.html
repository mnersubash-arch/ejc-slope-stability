    Critical surface (search)

    Finds the circle (2D) and the sphere centred on y = 0 (3D) with the lowest accepted FoS for the current inputs, mesh and grid option. The search tries surfaces that leave the ground at a front exit (the toe or the face; with the base-circle option also the pit floor) and at a back exit (the face or the crest), and that meet the ground at both exits, so the mass has no vertical back face.

    EJC against GHB, 3D against 2D

    The EJC (Moscarda closure, at the current ν) and GHB on the current surface, mesh and inputs, in 3D and 2D, with their ratios.

    Back-analysis

    The value of one input at which the FoS equals a target (F = 1 for a failure), found by bisection between two values where F is above and below the target, on the prescribed surface.

    Parameter sweep

    The FoS as one input varies over evenly spaced values, for the strength models and dimensions ticked. The other inputs stay as they are.

    Monte Carlo: probability of failure

    Each input below is drawn from a normal distribution with the current value as its mean and the standard deviation you enter (0 keeps it fixed), cut off at its valid range. No standard deviation is assumed: enter them from your site data. The FoS of every sample is solved with the current model; Pf = P(F < 1) among the accepted results.

    This page solves with engine.js, a line-for-line port of ejc_app/engine.py.
